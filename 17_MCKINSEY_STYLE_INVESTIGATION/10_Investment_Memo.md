18. FINAL VERDICT

Investment Rating:

Thesis:

Recrivio appears well-positioned to benefit from long-term demand for
technology talent and workforce enablement.

However, its ultimate enterprise value will depend on whether it successfully
evolves from a recruiter-centric services model into a technology-enabled
talent intelligence platform with recurring relationships, operational
leverage, and defensible competitive advantages.

The company's future is likely to be determined less by the number of resumes
it processes and more by the quality of the systems, data, and strategic
capabilities it builds around talent.
