# Executive Overview

RecriVio is an AI-powered Recruitment Intelligence Platform designed to modernize talent acquisition through intelligent automation, recruitment analytics, predictive hiring insights, and executive dashboards.

Unlike conventional Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS), which primarily focus on candidate management, RecriVio extends the recruitment lifecycle by integrating AI-assisted resume intelligence, recruitment analytics, recruiter productivity measurement, hiring intelligence, executive reporting, and decision-support dashboards into a unified platform.

The project combines software engineering, artificial intelligence, data analytics, and recruitment domain research to demonstrate how organizations can transition from operational hiring to data-driven talent acquisition.

---

# Business Problem

Modern recruitment teams face increasing operational complexity due to high application volumes, evolving skill requirements, and growing expectations for faster hiring decisions.

### Key Industry Challenges

| Challenge                         | Business Impact              |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| High application volume           | Increased recruiter workload |
| Manual resume screening           | Longer hiring cycles         |
| Inconsistent candidate evaluation | Reduced hiring quality       |
| Limited recruitment analytics     | Poor executive visibility    |
| Skills shortages                  | Increased hiring costs       |
| Fragmented hiring systems         | Operational inefficiency     |

Industry research indicates that organizations continue to face persistent talent shortages, while many HR teams are adopting AI and automation to improve recruitment efficiency and quality-of-hire. AI-assisted recruiting is increasingly being used for resume screening, sourcing, and workflow automation.
