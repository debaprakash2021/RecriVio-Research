# RecriVio Screening Framework

## Founder-Level Multi-Stage Candidate Intelligence & Evaluation Architecture

> **Mission:** Build a transparent, evidence-driven, AI-assisted screening pipeline that progressively filters candidates through increasingly expensive evaluation stages while maximizing fairness, accuracy, explainability, and hiring success.

# Founder Summary

Screening should not be viewed as a binary filtering mechanism but as a **multi-stage intelligence pipeline** that transforms raw candidate data into evidence-based hiring decisions. The optimal architecture combines deterministic eligibility rules, semantic AI matching, competency evaluation, explainable scoring, human oversight for uncertain cases, and continuous learning from hiring outcomes. Over time, this creates a self-improving screening engine that simultaneously increases recruiter productivity, improves candidate quality, and strengthens RecriVio's long-term competitive advantage.
