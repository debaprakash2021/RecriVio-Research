# Founder Summary

Joining and onboarding should be engineered as an **intelligent continuation of the hiring pipeline**, not as a disconnected HR process. Every interaction—from preboarding verification to 90-day performance—should generate structured signals that improve future sourcing, screening, scoring, and hiring decisions. The result is a closed-loop talent system where operational excellence directly strengthens RecriVio’s AI models, retention rates, recruiter trust, and long-term competitive advantage.
