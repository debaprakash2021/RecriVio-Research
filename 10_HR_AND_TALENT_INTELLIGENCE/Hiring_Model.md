# RecriVio Hiring Model

## Founder-Level AI-Native Hiring Operating System & Decision Intelligence Architecture

> **Mission:** Design a hiring model that consistently identifies high-potential candidates through a structured, explainable, AI-assisted, and continuously learning pipeline. The objective is not merely to fill vacancies but to maximize long-term organizational performance while reducing hiring risk, time-to-fill, and recruiter workload.

# 23. Founder Summary

RecriVio's Hiring Model should operate as an **AI-native organizational decision system**, not a sequence of recruiter tasks. Every stage—from workforce planning and sourcing to interviews, offers, onboarding, and post-hire performance—should generate structured intelligence that feeds back into the platform. The end goal is a continuously improving hiring engine capable of reducing hiring risk, increasing workforce quality, accelerating recruitment cycles, and creating a proprietary talent intelligence advantage that compounds with every successful hire.
