# Executive Summary

Revenue generation at Recrivio should not be viewed as a single transaction between recruiter and client.

Instead, it is a multi-stage value creation pipeline where commercial activities, recruitment execution, and customer success work together to convert business demand into realized revenue.

The company's financial health depends not only on winning new clients but also on successfully converting hiring mandates into completed joins, collecting payments efficiently, and expanding long-term customer relationships.

# 2. Stage-by-Stage Financial Analysis

## Stage 1 – Lead Generation

Objective:
Acquire organizations with hiring demand.

Activities:
- Founder outreach
- LinkedIn networking
- Referrals
- Enterprise sales
- Partnerships

Revenue:
₹0

Cost:
Marketing + Business Development

KPI:
Cost per Qualified Lead (CQL)

---

## Stage 2 – Discovery

Objective:
Understand hiring requirements.

Activities:
- Stakeholder meetings
- Role clarification
- Technical discussions
- Budget alignment

Revenue:
₹0

Risk:
Poor requirement gathering leads to poor candidate quality.

---

## Stage 3 – Commercial Agreement

Outputs:
- Scope
- Fee structure
- SLA
- Payment terms
- Replacement policy

Revenue:
Potential

No cash realized yet.

---

## Stage 4 – Hiring Execution

This is the largest operational cost center.

Activities:
- Market mapping
- Candidate sourcing
- Outreach
- Resume review
- Screening
- Follow-ups

Consumes:
- Recruiter time
- ATS resources
- Technology infrastructure

---

## Stage 5 – Candidate Presentation

Shortlisted candidates are submitted.

Important KPI:

Submission-to-Interview Ratio

A low ratio indicates poor sourcing quality.

---

## Stage 6 – Interview Pipeline

Activities:
- Scheduling
- Candidate preparation
- Feedback loops
- Stakeholder coordination

Risk:
Candidate drop-off.

Every failed interview increases delivery cost.

---

## Stage 7 – Offer Management

Critical risks:

- Counter offers
- Compensation mismatch
- Delayed approvals
- Candidate ghosting

This is often one of the highest-risk stages in recruitment economics.

---

## Stage 8 – Candidate Joining

This is typically the economic trigger.

Only after successful joining does placement revenue become realizable.

This transforms operational effort into billable value.

---

## Stage 9 – Invoice

Accounts team issues invoice.

Possible payment structures:

- Immediate
- Net 30
- Net 45
- Net 60

Longer payment cycles negatively impact working capital.

---

## Stage 10 – Cash Collection

Revenue is economically earned only after payment collection.

Metrics:

- Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
- Collection efficiency
- Bad debt ratio

Cash timing matters as much as contract value.

---

# 3. Revenue Leakage Points

Revenue is lost when:

- Leads do not convert.
- Clients cancel mandates.
- Candidates reject offers.
- Candidates do not join.
- Invoices remain unpaid.
- Clients churn.

Each leakage point should be monitored separately.

# 9. Strategic Observation

The highest-value stage in the pipeline is not sourcing.

It is the successful conversion of hiring mandates into completed joins followed by repeat engagements.

This is where long-term enterprise value is created.

---

# 10. Founder-Level Insight

From an investor's perspective, the strongest version of Recrivio is not one that closes the most placements.

It is one that systematically converts customer trust into recurring engagements, technology-enabled efficiency, and expanding lifetime value while minimizing revenue leakage across the hiring lifecycle.
