**Document Version:** v1.0
**Research Type:** Economic Reverse Engineering

# Executive Summary

The central hypothesis of this research is that Recrivio is not a single-revenue business.

Instead, it appears to follow a hybrid monetization strategy combining recruitment services, workforce enablement, and adjacent HR operations.

This diversification reduces dependence on one-time hiring transactions and creates opportunities for recurring customer relationships.

---

# 2. Revenue Stream A – Permanent Recruitment

## Description

The client engages Recrivio to identify and deliver qualified candidates for full-time employment.

Revenue Event:
Candidate joins successfully.

Industry Pricing:
- Percentage of annual CTC
OR
- Fixed placement fee

Characteristics:
- High-value
- One-time
- Performance-linked

Confidence:
HIGH

---

# 3. Revenue Stream B – Recruitment Process Outsourcing (RPO)

## Description

Instead of filling one role, Recrivio may become an external recruitment function for multiple positions.

Revenue Event:
Monthly engagement or contractual retainer.

Characteristics:
- Predictable revenue
- Longer customer relationships
- Higher switching costs

Confidence:
MEDIUM

---

# 4. Revenue Stream C – Employer of Record (EOR)

## Description

International companies hiring talent in India may require a legal employment intermediary.

Recrivio could potentially:
- Manage employment contracts
- Handle payroll
- Ensure statutory compliance

Revenue Event:
Monthly service fee per employee.

Characteristics:
- Recurring
- Sticky
- Operationally intensive

Confidence:
MEDIUM

# 10. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV)

Weak Client:

1 hire
→ engagement ends

High-Value Client:

10 hires
→ payroll support
→ future mandates
→ referrals
→ strategic partnership

LTV grows exponentially with retained relationships.

# 11. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

Likely components:

- Founder-led sales
- Business development
- Marketing
- Events
- LinkedIn outreach
- Enterprise networking
- Sales salaries

Goal:

LTV >> CAC

Otherwise, growth becomes uneconomical.

---

# 12. Gross Margin Considerations

Largest cost driver:

Human capital.

Revenue scales through:
- Better recruiter productivity
- Automation
- Standardized workflows
- AI-assisted sourcing

Unlike pure SaaS businesses, staffing models generally have lower gross margins due to labor intensity.

# 14. Strategic Risk Analysis

Revenue concentration risks:

- Dependence on hiring market cycles
- Economic downturns reducing hiring
- Client churn
- AI commoditizing sourcing
- Margin pressure from competition

Mitigation:

Expand recurring operational services.

---

# 16. CEO-Level Insight

The most valuable customer is not the one paying the highest placement fee today.

It is the customer that repeatedly entrusts hiring, workforce operations, and long-term talent strategy to Recrivio over multiple years.

Therefore, maximizing retention and cross-service adoption is likely more important than maximizing short-term placement volume.
