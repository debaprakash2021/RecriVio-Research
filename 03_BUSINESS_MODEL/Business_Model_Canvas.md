# 1. Executive Thesis

Recrivio should not be analyzed as a traditional recruitment consultancy.

The company appears to operate as a **Talent Infrastructure Platform**, combining recruitment expertise, technology, operational execution, and workforce enablement into a unified service model.

The fundamental value proposition is not "finding candidates."

The fundamental value proposition is:

> Helping organizations reduce the time, uncertainty, and operational complexity involved in building high-quality teams.
