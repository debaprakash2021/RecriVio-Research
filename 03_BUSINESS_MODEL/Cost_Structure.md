# Executive Summary

Recrivio appears to operate as a service-intensive HRTech business.

Unlike pure SaaS companies, where software is the primary cost center, Recrivio's largest expense is expected to be human capital. Consequently, profitability depends not only on increasing revenue but also on improving recruiter productivity, process efficiency, and technology leverage.

The company's long-term objective should be to increase revenue per employee without proportionally increasing operating costs.

# 2. Cost Classification

## Fixed Costs

Remain relatively stable regardless of hiring volume.

Examples:
- Leadership salaries
- Office rent
- Software subscriptions
- Legal & compliance
- HR & finance staff
- Brand marketing
- Cloud infrastructure
- Insurance
- Corporate administration

Characteristics:
- Paid even during hiring slowdowns.
- Improve economies of scale when spread across more revenue.

---

## Variable Costs

Increase with business volume.

Examples:
- Recruiter compensation tied to delivery
- Sales commissions
- Candidate sourcing expenses
- Third-party recruitment tools
- Background verification charges
- Travel for client engagements
- Event participation

Characteristics:
- Scale with customer activity.
- Easier to control than fixed costs.

# 5. Largest Structural Constraint

Unlike software companies:

Revenue ≠ Automatic Scalability

If every additional client requires hiring another recruiter, margins remain constrained.

Therefore, sustainable growth depends on increasing:

Revenue Generated Per Recruiter.

---

Potential automation targets:

- Resume parsing
- Candidate ranking
- JD matching
- Interview scheduling
- Follow-up reminders
- CRM updates
- Analytics reporting

Every automated hour reduces labor cost and increases recruiter capacity.

# 8. Idle Capacity Risk

Recruiters without active mandates generate cost but no revenue.

Similarly:

- Underutilized sales staff
- Low ATS usage
- Excess office capacity

all reduce operating efficiency.

Utilization management is therefore critical.

Ranked by expected impact:

# 11. Strategic Observation

The highest-performing recruitment firms are not necessarily those with the largest teams.

They are those that maximize:

Output Per Employee

through process excellence and technology enablement.

---

# 12. Founder-Level Hypothesis

As Recrivio scales, its competitive advantage should shift from:

"More recruiters"

to

"Smarter recruiters supported by superior systems, data, and automation."

This transition is essential for improving margins and defending against lower-cost competitors.

---

# 13. Risks

- Recruiter attrition
- Rising salary costs
- Heavy dependence on manual effort
- Increased customer acquisition costs
- Hiring slowdowns during economic downturns
- Underinvestment in automation
