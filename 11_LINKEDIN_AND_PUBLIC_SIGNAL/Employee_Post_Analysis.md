# Founder Summary

Employee-generated content should be viewed as a **distributed trust infrastructure** rather than a social media tactic. The highest-value posts are authentic technical explanations, engineering learnings, research insights, hiring experiences, and educational content that demonstrate competence without overt promotion. By empowering employees to share expertise responsibly, RecriVio creates a compounding network effect where individual professional credibility reinforces corporate reputation, improves employer branding, attracts exceptional talent, and establishes the company as a long-term authority in AI-driven career intelligence.
