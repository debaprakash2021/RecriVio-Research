# Founder Summary

The RecriVio founder's LinkedIn presence should function as a **strategic executive intelligence platform**, not a personal branding exercise. Each post should convert firsthand product experience, engineering decisions, market observations, and research into durable public knowledge. Over time, this creates a compounding asset: stronger founder credibility leads to stronger company trust, which attracts better talent, deeper partnerships, enterprise opportunities, and sustained competitive advantage. The founder should be recognized not because they post frequently, but because every post raises the quality of industry discourse.
