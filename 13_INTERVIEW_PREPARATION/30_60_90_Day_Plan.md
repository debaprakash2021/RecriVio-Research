# RecriVio 30-60-90 Day Strategic Execution Framework

## Founder-Level Operating System for Engineering, Product, AI, Leadership & Organizational Execution

> **Mission:** Create a repeatable execution framework that enables rapid understanding, disciplined prioritization, measurable delivery, continuous learning, and strategic leadership during the first 90 days of any major responsibility. This document represents the operational mindset of the RecriVio founder rather than a traditional HR onboarding plan.

# Founder Summary

The first 90 days should never be viewed as an onboarding period—they are an opportunity to establish a long-term execution system. The RecriVio operating philosophy emphasizes understanding before optimization, evidence before decisions, systems before shortcuts, and measurable outcomes before recognition. Whether acting as a founder, engineer, or future engineering leader, success is achieved by rapidly acquiring context, delivering visible value, documenting knowledge, building scalable processes, and continuously improving both the product and the organization.
