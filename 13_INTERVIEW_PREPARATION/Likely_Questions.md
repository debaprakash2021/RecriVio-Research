# FINAL PRINCIPLE

The purpose of this document is **not to memorize answers**.

Its purpose is to ensure that every important question about RecriVio has already been anticipated, researched, and supported by evidence elsewhere in the repository.

A strong interview is rarely about having perfect answers. It is about demonstrating structured thinking, sound engineering judgment, evidence-based reasoning, and the ability to communicate complex ideas clearly under pressure.
