## FQ-001

### Why should RecriVio exist if LinkedIn already dominates professional networking?

**Purpose**

Challenge the core reason for RecriVio's existence.

---

**Hidden Assumption**

LinkedIn has already solved hiring.

---

**Evidence Required**

* AI capability comparison
* ATS limitations
* Decision intelligence gaps
* Skills graph limitations
* Recruiter workflow analysis
* Candidate experience comparison

---

**Weak Answer**

> LinkedIn is different.

---

**Founder-Level Answer**

> LinkedIn organizes professionals. RecriVio aims to improve hiring decisions. Rather than competing as a professional network, RecriVio focuses on semantic understanding, explainable AI, evidence-based candidate evaluation, recruiter intelligence, and decision support. The strategic objective is to improve hiring quality rather than simply increase candidate visibility.

---

**Repository References**

* Competitive Intelligence
* Candidate Scoring System
* Hiring Model
* Case Studies
* Moats

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

A founder should never aim to have immediate answers to every difficult question.

Instead, the objective is to build a disciplined system for asking better questions, validating assumptions with evidence, documenting decisions transparently, and continuously refining the company's strategy as new information emerges.

The strength of RecriVio will not come from avoiding scrutiny—it will come from developing the capability to withstand, learn from, and improve through rigorous examination.
