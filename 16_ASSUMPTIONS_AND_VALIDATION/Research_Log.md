# Research Log

**Project:** RecriVio – AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Platform

**Document Type:** Research Intelligence Log

**Classification:** Internal R&D Document

**Purpose**

This document is the central evidence repository for RecriVio. Unlike a traditional research diary, it records how research findings influenced product architecture, business strategy, AI design, and technical decisions.

Every major decision should be traceable to evidence rather than assumption.

# Closing Principle

```text
Research does not end when a feature is implemented.

Research ends only when assumptions have been validated,
decisions are supported by evidence,
and continuous monitoring confirms that those decisions remain correct.

Every architecture decision inside RecriVio should be explainable,
traceable,
and supported by verifiable evidence.
```
