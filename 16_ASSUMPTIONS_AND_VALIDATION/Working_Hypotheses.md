# Working Hypotheses

**Project:** RecriVio – AI-Powered Recruitment Intelligence Platform

**Document Type:** Strategic Research Hypotheses Framework

**Classification:** Internal Product Research • Living Validation Document

**Purpose**

This document captures the core hypotheses that drive the evolution of RecriVio. These are not product requirements or feature requests—they are assumptions that require continuous validation through customer research, technical experimentation, market analysis, and measurable outcomes.

Every hypothesis must eventually be classified as:

```text
Validated
Modified
Rejected
Deferred
```
