# AI Guardrails

Mandatory controls before production deployment.

□ Human approval required for hiring recommendations.

□ Every AI decision must be explainable.

□ Every prediction must include a confidence category.

□ Model versions must be traceable.

□ User feedback must be captured for continuous improvement.

□ Sensitive candidate data must follow privacy regulations.

# AI Operating Principles

```text
Build fewer models.

Build better decisions.

Measure business outcomes.

Treat explainability as a product feature.

Protect candidate trust.

Continuously validate model behaviour.

Keep humans accountable.

Use AI to augment expertise, never to replace responsibility.
```

---

# Closing Directive

```text
The objective of RecriVio is not to become an AI-powered recruitment platform.

The objective is to become a trusted recruitment intelligence company where artificial intelligence is an enabling capability, not the product itself.

Every AI investment should increase confidence, transparency, and business value while preserving human judgment at every critical hiring decision.
```
