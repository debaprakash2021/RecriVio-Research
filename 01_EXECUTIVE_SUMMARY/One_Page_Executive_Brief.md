# Key Things To NOT Forget In The Interview

1. **They are NOT a traditional placement agency** — call them a "talent infrastructure company" or "talent platform"
2. **GTM is a new service line** — show you understand it's a strategic expansion, not just sales
3. **The founder is values-driven** — reference his story about underrepresented talent when asked "Why Recrivio?"
4. **They have an AI roadmap** — mention AI-powered screening and EOR platform as exciting developments
5. **HSR Layout Bengaluru** — this matters; it's a startup hub. They opened a new office in 2025, showing growth momentum
