# 2. The 30-Second Pitch (What Makes Them Different)

> Most recruitment agencies give you resumes. Most EOR platforms give you compliance. Most consulting firms give you strategy. Recrivio gives you all three — and that's the differentiator.
>
> They're a Bengaluru-based talent infrastructure company that sits between a startup's growth ambitions and the talent required to execute them. For global clients (US, Europe), they solve the "how do we hire in India without setting up an entity" problem. For domestic startups, they solve the "how do we hire faster and smarter" problem. And now with their GTM consulting practice, they're solving "how do we build revenue strategy while scaling the team" — all under one roof.

Use this when: elevator ride, networking event, warm-up question in interview.

# 3. The 60-Second Pitch (Full Context)

> Recrivio is a Bengaluru-based company built by Krishna Chaturvedi, who spent years inside Google, Microsoft, and Autodesk before starting this venture. His insight was simple but powerful: the best founders in India are being slowed down not by their product — but by the complexity of building teams.
>
> Recrivio attacks this problem across six dimensions: talent consulting for active hiring, IT staffing for on-demand technical resources, an EOR platform for global companies hiring in India, automated background verification, payroll and compliance management, and now GTM consulting to help startup clients actually generate revenue from the teams they're building.
>
> They've worked with 40+ companies — including names like Rapido, Zomato, and EXL — and placed 500+ candidates, many from underrepresented Tier 2 and Tier 3 cities. The underlying mission is making hiring equitable and building a system where the right person meets the right opportunity regardless of where they come from.

> What excites me about this company is that they're at the exact inflection point where startup culture meets systemic scale-up — and that's where I want to be.

Use this when: "Tell me about the company" in a formal interview. End with the last line to pivot to YOU.

# 4. The "Why Recrivio" Pitch (Interview Specific)

> Three things stand out about Recrivio for me.
>
> First, the business model. Unlike pure staffing agencies, Recrivio is building toward a full-stack talent infrastructure platform — and the GTM consulting layer is a genuinely differentiated wedge. I've studied how they've positioned it relative to Uplers, Deel, and Zyoin, and the full-stack play is something competitors haven't combined.
>
> Second, the founder's philosophy. Krishna Chaturvedi has said publicly that every candidate who gets their first job is a personal win for him. That kind of founder-led mission tends to produce a different culture than a company that only optimizes for billing.
>
> Third, the stage. Series 0-to-1 is where the most learning happens. I'd rather be the person who helps figure out how to scale a GTM motion than join a company where the process is already rigid. That's what this role is — and that's where I want to be early in my career.

Use this when: "Why do you want to join Recrivio?" — the gold standard answer.

# 5. The Competitive Context Pitch (Shows Deep Research)

> I looked at how Recrivio competes with the four main categories of competitors.
>
> Against Uplers — which has 3.5M+ candidates and AI-powered matching — Recrivio's advantage is the founder-relationship model and the GTM consulting layer. Uplers is a marketplace. Recrivio is a partner.
>
> Against Deel and Remote — which charge $599/month per employee for EOR — Recrivio's advantage is that it's India-specialized and significantly more cost-competitive for startups that don't need 150-country global coverage.
>
> Against Quess and TeamLease — massive listed companies with 480,000+ contractors — Recrivio's advantage is startup agility. You're not getting a dedicated account manager from Quess if you're a 20-person company. At Recrivio, the founder might personally follow up.
>
> Against SutraHR and Zyoin — which are direct competitors in startup recruitment — Recrivio's advantage is the expanding ecosystem around talent: EOR, payroll, BGV, GTM consulting. More touch points means higher client stickiness.

Use this when: technical interview rounds, or when asked "what do you know about our competitive landscape?"
