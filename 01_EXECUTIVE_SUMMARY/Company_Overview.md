# 3. The Core Problem Recrivio Solves

## 3.1 The Problem Statement

Most startups and fast-growing tech companies face a universal bottleneck:

```
They grow in product faster than they can grow in people.
```

Specifically:
- Hiring the right technical and business talent is slow (average time-to-hire: 45–90 days)
- Evaluating candidate quality requires domain expertise most founders lack
- Managing global payroll, compliance, and contracts while building product is a distraction
- Background verification adds another layer of operational overhead
- Most staffing agencies deliver resumes — not insight, not speed, not accountability

## 3.2 Recrivio's Intervention

Recrivio compresses ALL of these problems into a single partner relationship:

```
                        ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                        │        CLIENT COMPANY         │
                        │   (Startup / Global Firm)     │
                        └──────────────┬────────────────┘

                        ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                        │           RECRIVIO            │
                        │    Talent Infrastructure      │

                               │               │
              ┌────────────────┘               └────────────────┐
              ▼                                                  ▼
    ┌─────────────────┐                             ┌─────────────────┐
    │  TALENT SIDE    │                             │  OPERATIONS SIDE │
    │ • Sourcing      │                             │ • EOR            │
```

**The strategic differentiation:** Most competitors do EITHER the left box OR the right box. Recrivio attempts to do both — positioning itself as a **full-stack talent partner**, not just a vendor.

# 4. Service Portfolio — What Recrivio Actually Delivers

## 4.1 IT Staffing
- On-demand access to pre-vetted IT and digital professionals
- Flexible staffing models: contract, contract-to-hire, full-time
- Specializations: Engineering, Product, AI, Data, Cloud, Full-Stack
- Client examples: Rapido, Zomato, EXL, Coforge, Persistent Systems, Hexaware, Amazon
- Positioning: "A partner mindset, not just a vendor tag"

## 4.2 Talent Consulting (Startup-Focused)
- Partners directly with founders and TA teams on active hiring mandates
- Builds candidate pipelines for Engineering, Product, AI, Sales, Operations, Leadership
- Conducts market intelligence and competitor hiring research
- Manages candidate experience and interview coordination
- Builds talent communities for long-term pipeline

## 4.3 GTM Consulting (New Service Line — 2025–26)
- Helps client startups build Go-To-Market strategies
- Analyzes sales performance, conversion funnels, and competitor movements
- Supports new market and customer segment expansion
- Builds dashboards, tracks pipeline health: Lead → Opportunity → Deal → Revenue
- Creates pitch decks, GTM narratives, and Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs)
- Works on high-priority strategic accounts

## 4.4 Employer of Record (EOR) — Platform in Development
- Enables global companies to hire Indian talent without setting up a local entity
- Handles employment contracts, payroll, compliance, benefits
- Building this as a SaaS-based platform (tech-first EOR, not just manual managed service)
- Competes in the same category as Deel, Remote.com, Multiplier — but India-specialized and lower cost

## 4.5 Background Verification (BGV)
- Automated verification platform: one dashboard, add candidates, run checks
- Employment verification, education checks, criminal record checks, identity verification
- Designed for fast-growing teams that need compliance without operational overhead

## 4.6 Payroll & Compliance
- Built-in payroll processing for contracted/employed talent
- Statutory compliance: PF, ESI, TDS, Professional Tax
- Contract and workflow automation

# 13. Why This Company Matters (Strategic Assessment)

**Bull Case for Recrivio:**
- Riding three massive waves: remote work, India talent boom, EOR market growth
- Founder with the right background (Big Tech) and the right mission (underrepresented talent)
- Full-stack approach creates high switching costs once a client uses 3+ services
- GTM consulting is a unique wedge no competitor has

**Bear Case:**
- Execution bandwidth limited at 100–200 person scale
- Uplers, Zyoin, SutraHR are better funded/established in the pure-recruitment layer
- EOR platform vs Deel is a David vs Goliath build
- Depends heavily on founder relationships for early-stage client acquisition

**Verdict:**
Recrivio is a high-upside early-stage bet on India's talent infrastructure market. The right candidate joining now gets:
- Direct founder access
- Fast career growth based on performance, not tenure
- Exposure to GTM, recruitment, and product strategy simultaneously
- Equity upside potential if the company scales
