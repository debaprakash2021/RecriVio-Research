# RecriVio Expansion Strategy

## Founder-Level Strategic Scaling Blueprint

> **Core Philosophy:** Expand only when the previous layer has achieved measurable product-market fit, positive unit economics, operational maturity, and a reusable playbook. Expansion should compound the platform’s network effects, data assets, and AI intelligence rather than dilute focus.

# Founder Summary

Expansion should never be measured by the number of countries entered or features released. It should be measured by the **compounding strength of RecriVio’s platform**: richer proprietary data, stronger AI models, deeper workflow integration, healthier unit economics, expanding network effects, and increasing trust. Every new market, product, or partnership must reinforce these core assets so that growth strengthens the business rather than fragmenting it.
