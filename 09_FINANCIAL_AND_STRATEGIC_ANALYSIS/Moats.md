# Founder Takeaway

The primary objective is **not** to protect features—it is to protect *compounding advantages*. RecriVio's enduring moat should emerge from proprietary hiring outcomes, continuously improving AI models, a living talent knowledge graph, embedded recruiter workflows, ecosystem integrations, and accumulated trust. As these layers reinforce one another, the cost of replicating the platform rises faster than the cost of copying any individual capability, creating a durable strategic advantage over conventional HRTech competitors.
