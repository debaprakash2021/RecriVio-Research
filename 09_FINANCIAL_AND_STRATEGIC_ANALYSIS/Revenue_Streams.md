# RecriVio Revenue Architecture

## Strategic Monetization Blueprint (Founder-Level Financial Design)

> **Objective:** Build a resilient, multi-layered revenue engine where no single income source contributes more than 40% of total ARR in the long term, reducing platform risk while maximizing valuation multiples.

# 8. Risk Diversification Matrix

| Risk                        | Mitigation                                            |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| AI inference cost inflation | Usage-based AI credits                                |
| Reduced hiring cycles       | Diversify into education and enterprise subscriptions |
| Competitive ATS market      | Differentiate with AI intelligence and APIs           |
| Dependence on job postings  | Expand recurring SaaS and analytics                   |
| Customer concentration      | Segment across B2C, SMB, Enterprise, Universities     |

---

# 9. Market-Aligned Strategic Observations

Current HRTech and AI recruiting trends increasingly favor:

* Subscription-led SaaS with freemium entry points.
* Cloud-native platforms that monetize through predictable recurring contracts.
* Consumption-based AI pricing for compute-intensive features.
* Enterprise demand for integrated analytics and API ecosystems.
* Diversified monetization beyond simple job-posting fees.

These dynamics align with RecriVio's proposed architecture of layered subscriptions, AI credit consumption, enterprise licensing, recruiter seats, APIs, and data products.
