# 3. Architectural Interpretation

## 3.1 Why is the founder's background important?

Founders usually build products that solve problems they have personally experienced.

Krishna Chaturvedi's experience inside large technology organizations likely exposed him to:

- Slow hiring cycles
- Difficulty evaluating engineering candidates
- Operational inefficiencies
- Global workforce coordination
- Scaling challenges

Rather than viewing recruitment as "finding resumes," his background suggests a systems-oriented perspective.

---

## 3.2 Inferred Design Philosophy

Based on public messaging, Recrivio appears to be designed around the following principles:

- Reduce hiring friction.
- Use technology to augment recruitment.
- Build long-term workforce infrastructure rather than transactional staffing.
- Support startups and global organizations with scalable talent solutions.

These are architectural inferences and should not be treated as officially confirmed company doctrine.
