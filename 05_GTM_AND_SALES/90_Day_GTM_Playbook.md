# 90-Day GTM Playbook – Recrivio
## Strategic Execution Plan for a GTM Consultant

Document Version: v1.0
Prepared As: Execution Blueprint
Research Type: Go-To-Market Operations

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EXECUTIVE OBJECTIVE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Mission:

Increase qualified pipeline generation, improve client acquisition efficiency,
and establish a scalable, data-driven GTM operating system while positioning
Recrivio as a strategic talent partner rather than a transactional recruiter.

North Star KPI:

Generate sustainable, repeatable pipeline growth while improving conversion
rates and customer lifetime value.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 1 (DAY 1–30)
"UNDERSTAND, MAP, AND BUILD THE FOUNDATION"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objective

Before selling anything, deeply understand:
- Customers
- Market
- Competitors
- Internal processes
- Existing GTM motion

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Internal GTM Audit
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Study:

- Existing sales funnel
- Lead sources
- CRM structure
- Sales scripts
- Outreach templates
- Client onboarding process
- Win/loss history

Deliverable:
"GTM System Audit Report"

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Build an ICP Database
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Target Segments:

- VC-backed startups
- SaaS companies
- AI startups
- FinTech firms
- Product companies
- Global companies hiring in India

For each account capture:

- Employee count
- Funding stage
- Hiring activity
- Tech stack
- Key decision makers
- Open positions
- Expansion signals

Deliverable:
Prioritized target account list.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Create a Hiring Signal Engine
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Monitor:

- Funding announcements
- Leadership hiring
- Career page expansion
- New office launches
- Product launches
- Engineering hiring spikes

These become outbound triggers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Competitor Intelligence

Track competitors on:

- Messaging
- Pricing
- Hiring focus
- LinkedIn content
- Service positioning
- AI adoption

Deliverable:
Weekly competitor brief.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Dashboard Creation

Track:

- Accounts researched
- Qualified leads
- Meetings booked
- Conversion rates
- Pipeline value
- Average response rate

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS METRICS (DAY 30)

✓ 500 researched accounts
✓ ICP framework established
✓ Competitor database completed
✓ Hiring signal tracker operational
✓ GTM dashboard live

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 2 (DAY 31–60)
"EXECUTE AND OPTIMIZE"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objective

Convert research into qualified business opportunities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Personalized Outbound Campaigns
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

No mass messaging.

Every outreach references:

- Company growth
- Hiring trend
- Funding event
- Business challenge

Goal:
Start conversations, not send pitches.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Publish Hiring Intelligence

Create:

- Monthly hiring reports
- Salary insights
- AI hiring trends
- Startup hiring maps

Purpose:
Build credibility before selling.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Account-Based Marketing (ABM)

Top 100 accounts receive:

- Customized research
- Personalized messaging
- Industry insights
- Relevant hiring recommendations

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Referral Flywheel

After successful engagements:

Request:

- Introductions
- Testimonials
- Referrals
- Case studies

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. GTM Experimentation

Run A/B tests for:

- Subject lines
- LinkedIn messages
- Email formats
- CTA styles
- Meeting requests

Track statistically significant improvements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS METRICS (DAY 60)

✓ Improved response rate
✓ Increased meetings booked
✓ Higher proposal conversion
✓ Repeatable outbound playbook
✓ Active content engine

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PHASE 3 (DAY 61–90)
"SCALE AND SYSTEMIZE"
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Objective

Build systems that continue producing value beyond individual effort.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. AI-Assisted Prospect Scoring

Rank accounts based on:

- Hiring urgency
- Funding status
- Growth trajectory
- Hiring volume
- Strategic fit

Prioritize highest-value opportunities.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. Executive Dashboards

Provide leadership with:

Commercial:
- Pipeline value
- Win rate
- CAC

Operational:
- Time-to-close
- Lead velocity

Strategic:
- ICP penetration
- Segment performance

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. Knowledge Repository

Document:

- Best outreach
- Objection handling
- Case studies
- Industry insights
- Playbooks

Institutional knowledge compounds over time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. Expansion Strategy

For existing clients:

Map opportunities for:

- Additional hiring
- RPO
- Strategic advisory
- Talent intelligence
- Cross-functional hiring

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. Quarterly GTM Review

Answer:

- Which channels work?
- Which ICP converts best?
- Which industries have highest LTV?
- Which objections appear most often?
- Where is pipeline leaking?

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS METRICS (DAY 90)

✓ Predictable outbound engine
✓ AI-assisted prioritization
✓ Improved pipeline quality
✓ Repeatable GTM documentation
✓ Executive reporting cadence established

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Customer

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BIGGEST GTM RISKS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Chasing low-quality leads.
2. Generic outreach.
3. Poor ICP definition.
4. Overreliance on one acquisition channel.
5. Weak follow-up discipline.
6. Lack of data-driven decision making.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FOUNDER-LEVEL RECOMMENDATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The first responsibility of a GTM Consultant should not be "selling."

It should be creating a repeatable commercial operating system that:

- identifies the right customers,
- reaches them with contextual insights,
- converts them through consultative engagement,
- and expands them into long-term strategic relationships.

The highest-performing GTM organizations win not because they contact the most
prospects, but because they consistently engage the right prospects with the
right message at the right time.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PERSONAL EXECUTION PHILOSOPHY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

If I joined Recrivio tomorrow, my goal would not be to maximize activity.

It would be to maximize signal.

Every interaction, dashboard, outreach campaign, and research artifact should
contribute to a scalable GTM engine that becomes smarter and more effective
over time.
