Document Version: v2.0

# Executive Summary

Recrivio's GTM strategy should not be understood as "selling recruitment services."

Its actual GTM mission is:

> Convert hiring pain into long-term strategic partnerships by positioning Recrivio as an extension of the client's talent acquisition capability.

The GTM engine therefore combines:
- Market intelligence,
- outbound sales,
- founder credibility,
- recruiter expertise,
- operational excellence,
- and long-term account expansion.

# 2. Total Addressable Market (TAM)

Potential market includes:

- Indian startups
- Global companies hiring in India
- SaaS businesses
- Product companies
- FinTech
- HealthTech
- AI startups
- Enterprise technology firms

Not every company is worth pursuing.

Therefore ICP filtering becomes essential.

---

# 3. Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

Recrivio should realistically prioritize:

- VC-funded startups
- Engineering-heavy businesses
- High-growth organizations
- Companies with recurring hiring demand
- Organizations lacking mature internal recruiting functions

These customers maximize long-term account value.

# 6. Qualification Framework

A prospect should be evaluated on:

- Active hiring demand
- Hiring urgency
- Technical hiring volume
- Budget availability
- Decision-making speed
- Repeat hiring probability
- Strategic fit

Only qualified accounts should consume recruiter resources.

# 7. Core GTM Messaging

Weak Positioning:

"We provide recruitment services."

Strong Positioning:

"We help high-growth companies eliminate hiring bottlenecks and accelerate execution through technology-enabled talent solutions."

The second message sells outcomes rather than activities.

# 10. GTM Bottlenecks

Likely constraints include:

- Poor prospect qualification
- Generic outreach
- Slow proposal turnaround
- Weak follow-up discipline
- Limited market intelligence
- Low personalization
- Candidate shortages in niche roles

Each bottleneck should be measured and optimized separately.

# 14. Strategic Conclusion

Recrivio should not compete on recruiter count or pricing.

Its strongest GTM strategy is to become the preferred growth partner for technology-driven companies by combining market intelligence, consultative selling, recruitment expertise, and operational excellence into a repeatable customer acquisition engine.

The long-term moat is not access to candidates.

It is trusted relationships, superior execution, proprietary process knowledge, and the ability to consistently convert hiring demand into business outcomes.
