# Executive Summary

One of the biggest mistakes in B2B sales is confusing the customer with the user.

For Recrivio, the customer is not "any company that hires."

The ideal customer is an organization where:
- Hiring delays directly impact revenue or product delivery.
- Internal recruiting capacity is insufficient.
- Leadership values speed and quality over the lowest possible cost.
- The business is expected to continue hiring over multiple quarters.

Therefore, Recrivio's ICP is defined by **business urgency and hiring complexity**, not merely by company size.

---

# 1. ICP Scoring Framework

Every potential client can be scored across eight dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Hiring Velocity | 20% | Number of roles opened monthly |
| Hiring Urgency | 15% | Business impact of vacancies |
| Engineering Intensity | 15% | Dependence on technical talent |
| Growth Stage | 10% | Funding and expansion trajectory |
| Repeat Hiring Potential | 15% | Likelihood of future mandates |
| Budget Maturity | 10% | Ability to pay premium fees |
| Decision Speed | 5% | Procurement efficiency |
| Strategic Partnership Potential | 10% | Scope for long-term engagement |

Maximum Score = 100

# 2. Tier-1 ICP (Highest Priority)

## Profile

- VC-backed startup
- 30–300 employees
- Recently funded
- Engineering-led
- Hiring 5–20 people per quarter

## Decision Makers

- Founder
- CEO
- CTO
- VP Engineering

## Pain Points

- Product roadmap blocked by hiring delays.
- Founders spending too much time recruiting.
- Difficulty sourcing experienced engineers.
- Pressure from investors to scale quickly.

## Buying Trigger

- New funding round.
- Product expansion.
- Team scaling.
- New market launch.

## ICP Score

95/100

# 5. Customers to Avoid (Anti-ICP)

## Small businesses hiring once every few years

Problems:
- Low lifetime value.
- Price sensitivity.
- Limited repeat revenue.

---

## Organizations selecting vendors solely on cost

Problems:
- Margin pressure.
- Weak loyalty.
- High churn risk.

---

## Clients with undefined job descriptions

Problems:
- Excess recruiter effort.
- Poor placement success.
- Delivery inefficiency.

---

## Companies with long procurement cycles but tiny hiring volumes

Problems:
- High acquisition cost.
- Slow revenue realization.

# 7. Buying Triggers

Strong signals that a company may need Recrivio:

- Recent funding announcement.
- 10+ open engineering roles.
- New office launch.
- Geographic expansion.
- Product launch.
- Hiring freeze lifted.
- Internal recruiter attrition.

These events should trigger outbound outreach.

---

# 8. Buying Objections

Common concerns:

"We already have recruiters."

Response:
Position Recrivio as a capacity multiplier.

---

"We only hire occasionally."

Response:
Offer flexible engagement rather than dedicated retainers.

---

"Your pricing is higher."

Response:
Reframe around cost of delayed hiring and poor hiring decisions.

---

"We use LinkedIn ourselves."

Response:
Highlight process expertise, market intelligence, and execution support.

# 9. Customer Economics

Best Customer

- High hiring frequency.
- Multiple departments hiring.
- Repeat engagement.
- Cross-service potential.
- Referral potential.

Worst Customer

- One role.
- High negotiation.
- Low urgency.
- Low budget.
- No repeat business.

# 11. ICP Qualification Checklist

Before pursuing a prospect, ask:

✓ Are they actively scaling?
✓ Do they hire technical talent?
✓ Is hiring business-critical?
✓ Can leadership make decisions quickly?
✓ Is there repeat hiring potential?
✓ Can Recrivio create measurable value?

If ≥5 answers are "Yes", the account is high priority.

---

# 12. Strategic Recommendation

Recrivio should prioritize companies where hiring speed directly influences business outcomes rather than pursuing every available hiring mandate.

By concentrating on high-growth organizations with recurring hiring needs, the company can improve:
- Revenue predictability,
- Recruiter utilization,
- Customer lifetime value,
- and long-term profitability.

This focus also strengthens positioning as a strategic talent partner instead of a transactional recruitment vendor.
